# Data licence

The data this project adds is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
That covers the article segmentation and field structure, the romanised headwords and
their OSBCT attestation, the reports, and any Spanish renderings.

It does not cover the dictionary's own text or the page scans. Those belong to the
Masoeyein Board of Scholar-Elders, the Ministry of Religious Affairs of Myanmar, and the
people who scanned them, all credited in the README. The OCR'd Burmese in
`articles.jsonl` is a machine reading of their text, and we do not relicense it.
